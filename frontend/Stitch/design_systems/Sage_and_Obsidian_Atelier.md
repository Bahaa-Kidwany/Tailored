## Brand & Style

This design system embodies bespoke executive quiet luxury, structured precision, and organic architectural restraint. Built for high-altitude decision makers and discerning practitioners, the aesthetic rejects sensory saturation, aggressive neon states, and disposable digital trends in favor of timeless tactile permanence.

The visual language draws heavily from contemporary Nordic and Japanese architectural pavilions: wide atmospheric fields, deliberate framing, tactile stone and mineral palettes, and unhurried typographic rhythm. It balances substantial obsidian weight against airy mineral sage washes, crafting an interface that feels like handling heavy-stock cotton paper bound within milled slate and brushed green marble.

Key design attributes:
- **Quiet Authority:** Confident, deliberate spacing with zero unnecessary decorative flourishes.
- **Architectural Rhythm:** Distinct monolithic planes separated by hair-thin stone transitions rather than aggressive drop shadows.
- **Organic Executive Restraint:** Grounded deep racing forest greens paired with natural lichen, bone ivory, and subtle emerald dispatch accents for intentional operational status.

## Colors

The chromatic structure balances mineral tranquility with grounding architectural darkness. Surfaces prioritize anti-glare matte finishes and high legibility without harsh black-and-white polarity.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#142e23` - British Racing Forest):** The anchor of focal interactions, dominant navigation landmarks, key headers, and core commitments.
- **Secondary (`#0d1513` - Obsidian Charcoal):** Structural foundation providing deep baseline weight, monolithic structural frames, and heavy button states.
- **Tertiary (`#00a86b` - Atelier Dispatch Emerald):** Used strictly as a focused chromatic accent—precision telemetry, live status pings, validated indicators, and tactical micro-actions. Never used for massive flooded backdrops.
- **Neutral (`#63756c` - Muted Lichen):** Secondary metadata, inactive states, column headers, and structural borders when paired with soft stone tints.
- **Canvas Base (`#e8efe9` to `#f3f6f3` - Mineral Sage):** The ambient canvas wash that absorbs screen harshness and eliminates eye strain.
- **Surfaces & Cards (`#ffffff` and `#fbfdfc` - Pure Alabaster & Crisp Ivory):** Elevated focal content cards layered directly over the mineral sage background.
- **Hairline Dividers (`#d8e2dc` - Stone Mist):** Micro-fine 1px boundaries maintaining spatial order.
- **Ink Typography (`#121815` - Deep Slate Ink):** Highest contrast editorial copy, numbers, and primary labels.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between literary editorial heritage and precise technical calculation.

- **Headlines & Editorial Statements (`Newsreader`):** Infuses warmth, authority, and quiet bespoke craft. Headings are set in gentle medium weights with slight negative tracking, evoking premium private publishing. Italic cuts are reserved sparingly for secondary architectural meta-notes or qualitative quotes.
- **Interface, Numerical, & Functional Text (`Manrope`):** Provides an ultra-crisp, highly legible geometric sans foundation for system architecture, complex data dense tables, UI action labels, and high-precision executive metrics.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy:** Styled in uppercase or tabular metrics with extended letter-spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to mimic stamped architectural blueprints and technical atelier engravings.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors architectural framing: sweeping open spaces punctuated by rhythmically arranged structures. The layout employs a 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by strict maximum content boundaries.

- **Desktop (1440px and above):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and generous `3rem` canvas margins. Maximum readable container constrained to `1280px` for structured dashboards and `840px` for long-form executive briefs.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, and `2rem` outer margin. Dynamic sidebars collapse into sleek obsidian off-canvas drawers.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, and `1.25rem` side margins. Multi-column cards stack linearly into single pure alabaster cards with hairline borders.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows deliberate structural steps. Micro components use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for compact density, while structural cards, sections, and headers breathe using `space-lg` and `space-xl` to reinforce visual stillness.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system outright rejects exaggerated dropshadows and glossy blurred skeuomorphism. Spatial depth is derived from tonal paper layering, subtle hairline definition, and soft diffuse ambient occlusions.

- **Tonal Stepping:** The deepest level is the `#e8efe9` Mineral Sage canvas. Atop this rests `#ffffff` Alabaster / `#fbfdfc` Crisp Ivory structural containers. Higher utility surfaces (toolbars, contextual floating docks) lift subtly via micro-contrasted borders.
- **Hairline Framing:** All elevated planes, cards, and section boundaries are defined by a crisp `1px` border in Stone Mist (`#d8e2dc`). Under dark mode or within obsidian containers, boundaries transition to a low-opacity white veil (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Ambient Shadow (The Atelier Shadow):** For modal sheets, executive pickers, and detached floating navigation, depth is conveyed through an ultra-soft, low-opacity shadow tinted with deep forest slate:
  - `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -12px rgba(18, 24, 21, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(18, 24, 21, 0.03);`
- **Dark Inversion Wells:** Important executive actions or focal operational summaries utilize the obsidian base (`#0d1513`), creating an intentional visual anchor that sinks into or crowns the light mineral landscape.

## Shapes

The design system establishes a high-contrast relationship between broad rectilinear architectural frames and smooth, organic pill-shaped touchpoints.

- **Container Enclosures (`rounded-xl` / 3rem):** Dashboard panels, elevated content clusters, and modal enclosures feature generously softened structural corners that counter the rigorous grid lines.
- **Interactive Controls & Indicators (`rounded-full` / Pill):** Primary action buttons, filter tags, dispatch indicators, search pills, and form toggles use pure cylindrical pill geometry. This creates an inviting tactile quality reminiscent of polished river stones and milled architectural hardware.
- **Inner Micro Components (`rounded-md` to `rounded-lg`):** Nested elements like input fields, selection lists, and data table rows use softer 8px to 16px radii to maintain harmonic proportion inside parent containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Executive Pill:** Solid British Racing Forest (`#142e23`) fill, pure white crisp typography (`#ffffff`), `rounded-full`, with `0.875rem` vertical and `1.75rem` horizontal padding. Hover shifts smoothly to Obsidian Charcoal (`#0d1513`).
- **Secondary Atelier Pill:** Pure Ivory background (`#fbfdfc`), `1px` Stone Mist border (`#d8e2dc`), Slate Ink typography (`#121815`). On hover, border shifts to Muted Lichen (`#63756c`) with a soft tint wash (`#e8efe9`).
- **Dispatch Action Pill:** High-priority execution button utilizing Emerald (`#00a86b`) with dark slate ink text (`#060a08`) for calculated operational precision.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), compact height (28px).
- Mineral sage tint background with deep racing forest text for standard tags.
- Dynamic Status Badges feature a 6px glowing solid emerald (`#00a86b`) or amber indicator dot flanked by tabular uppercase tracking text in Muted Lichen (`#63756c`).

### Cards & Panels
- Background: Alabaster White (`#ffffff`) or Ivory (`#fbfdfc`).
- Border: `1px solid #d8e2dc`.
- Radius: `rounded-xl`.
- Padding: `2rem` internal layout padding with zero internal drop shadow unless floating. Header tiers within cards feature a Newsreader serif heading paired with uppercase Manrope metadata.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) search bars and `rounded-lg` multiline fields.
- Background: Soft ivory tint (`#fbfdfc`) surrounded by a hairline Stone Mist frame (`#d8e2dc`).
- Typography: Deep Slate Ink (`#121815`) input text with Lichen (`#63756c`) placeholder values.
- Focus state: Smooth transition to a `1px` primary forest green ring (`#142e23`) with a subtle `2px` diffuse forest mist glow (`rgba(20, 46, 35, 0.08)`). No harsh browser outlines.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Radio: Circular pill style with 2px inset stone border; selected state fills with `#142e23` containing an ivory pip.
- Checkbox: Soft squircle (`4px` radius) with `1.5px` border in `#63756c`. Active state features a solid British Racing Forest fill and clean white check mark.

### Lists & Atelier Data Grids
- Clean horizontal rows separated strictly by hairline dividers (`1px solid #d8e2dc`).
- Alternating hover states apply a mineral sage tint (`#f3f6f3`) without harsh shifts.
- Numerical columns strictly aligned using tabular figures in Manrope; row titles rendered in Newsreader or Medium Manrope.

### Contextual Floating Dock
- An elevated, pill-shaped command rail or global tab navigation that hovers over the bottom center of the interface.
- Matte Obsidian Charcoal (`#0d1513`) base with crisp Ivory icons and labels, punctuated by subtle Dispatch Emerald active pips.
