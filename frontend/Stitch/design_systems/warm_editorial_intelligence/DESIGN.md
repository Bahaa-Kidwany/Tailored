## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an AI-native career advisory partner designed for discerning professionals navigating complex career transitions. Moving away from the hyper-synthetic, dark-mode terminal aesthetics typical of generative AI, this system adopts an executive, literary warmth reminiscent of architectural monographs, bespoke bespoke tailoring ateliers, and high-end print publishing. 

The aesthetic is grounded in **Warm Editorial Modernism**:
- **Personality:** Authoritative, empathetic, highly discerning, and impeccably composed.
- **Target Audience:** Mid-to-executive level candidates, specialists, and modern professionals seeking strategic career guidance over noisy volume-hiring portals.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires clarity, poise, high agency, and deliberate focus. The interface should feel like stepping into a sunlit, oak-lined library equipped with state-of-the-art computational tools.
- **Core Aesthetic Tenet:** Textural depth through alabaster canvases, crisp hairline borders in warm stone and champagne, rich espresso/slate typography for peerless legibility, and targeted warmth through earthen terracotta and deep indigo ink.

## Colors

The palette establishes an intellectual, tactile warmth without forfeiting modern computational crispness.

- **Primary (`#C25E3E` - Terracotta Sienna):** A grounded, sun-baked earth tone used for definitive user actions, high-intent interactive states, and AI match-state emphasis. Avoid neon saturation; maintain mineral authenticity.
- **Secondary (`#1E2B45` - Deep Indigo Ink):** An archival, structured ink tone that provides executive weight for secondary actions, strategic tags, semantic status anchors, and specialized metadata badges.
- **Tertiary (`#D8B18A` - Champagne Dust):** A refined metallic warmth used sparingly for subtle highlights, active card borders, premium tier annotations, and gentle decorative accents.
- **Neutral Surface & Text (`#1F1D1B` - Deep Espresso):** Replaces harsh jet black with rich, natural umber-tinted slate for body and headline typography, preserving ultra-high contrast (exceeding WCAG AAA) against warm canvas tones.
- **Canvas & Architectural Foundation:**
  - Base canvas sits at `#FAF8F5` (Alabaster Cream).
  - Elevated surfaces rest on `#FFFFFF` (Pure Studio White) layered over `#F3EFEA` (Warm Stone Tint).
  - Hairline dividers utilize `#E8E2D9` (Champagne Stone) for delicate visual separation.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the human geometric approachability of Plus Jakarta Sans with the surgical precision of JetBrains Mono for agentic reasoning layers.

- **Primary Editorial Type (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its clear geometric geometry softened by humanist grotesque curves. Headings feature negative letter-spacing for compact, magazine-like impact. Body typography preserves open apertures for sustained readability during long-form role evaluations and conversational AI output.
- **Monospace Technical Anchor (`JetBrains Mono`):** Dedicated to technical metadata, match percentages, compensation ranges, salary bands, timestamp benchmarks, and agent action logs. It transforms dense structured metrics into legible data ribbons without cluttering the editorial flow.
- **Hierarchy Guidelines:**
  - Display and major headlines rely on medium and semi-bold weights rather than heavy black weights, sustaining an air of restraint and sophistication.
  - Monospace labels must always run in uppercase when functioning as metadata tags, role category markers, or system status indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model employs a responsive 12-column fluid grid system pinned to a maximum canvas container width of 1440px to ensure wide displays maintain editorial proportion and avoid conversational drift.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid with a `3rem` canvas margin and `2rem` column gutters. Dedicated multi-pane layouts: persistent agent command drawer (3 columns), contextual discovery stream (5 columns), and live role preview/synthesis sheet (4 columns).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with a `2rem` margin and `1.5rem` gutters. Sliding secondary panels and stacked executive overviews.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid with `1.25rem` screen margins and `1rem` gutters. Single-column linear flow prioritizing chat-to-card transitions with expandable bottom sheets for document inspect views.
- **Rhythm & Cadence:** Vertical spacing enforces breathing room around dense data clusters. Content containers utilize generous internal paddings (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) paired with disciplined micro-spacing (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) for internal pill groups, metadata pairs, and badge clusters.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons deep blurred dropshadows in favor of tonal layering, warm ambient diffusion, and translucent frosted glass scrims.

- **Tonal Layering:** 
  - Level 0 (Base Canvas): `#FAF8F5` (Alabaster Cream).
  - Level 1 (Card/Container Surfaces): `#FFFFFF` framed by a 1px solid `#E8E2D9` hairline border.
  - Level 2 (Floating Inspect Panels & Flyouts): `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border of `#DECDBA`.
- **Frosted Glass Scrim:**
  - Used for fixed navigation headers, AI reasoning progress trays, and contextual input bars hovering at the viewport base.
  - Specifications: Background `#FAF8F5` at 82% opacity with a `16px` backdrop-blur filter and an underside hairline border of `rgba(232, 226, 217, 0.8)`.
- **Ambient Micro-Elevations:**
  - Micro-lift (Hover cards & interactive modules): `0px 4px 16px -2px rgba(45, 38, 30, 0.04), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(45, 38, 30, 0.02)`.
  - Floating dialogs & active flyouts: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(45, 38, 30, 0.06), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(45, 38, 30, 0.03)`.
  - Shadows are strictly tinted with warm umber (`rgba(45, 38, 30)`)—never pure cold carbon or gray.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a curated **Soft** shape geometry (`roundedness: 1`). This maintains structural architectural clarity with 0.25rem (`4px`) baseline micro-radii, 0.5rem (`8px`) card container radii, and 0.75rem (`12px`) modal/canvas sheet corners.

- **Component Corners:** Standard inputs, buttons, chips, and data frames utilize `0.375rem` (6px) or `0.5rem` (8px). This creates tailored, sharp, and intentional silhouettes that eschew childish hyper-rounded bubbles.
- **Pill Shapes:** Strictly reserved for status chips, match scores, and category tags to differentiate interactive structural cards from atomic semantic badges.
- **Borders:** All primary and secondary components share a consistent `1px` structural hairline border, creating an orderly grid alignment reminiscent of bespoke ledger lines.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Terracotta Sienna (`#C25E3E`) fill with `#FFFFFF` text in Plus Jakarta Sans SemiBold. Crisp `0.375rem` radius. Subtle hover state transitioning to `#A84F32` without scaling gimmicks.
- **Secondary:** Alabaster White (`#FFFFFF`) surface with a 1px warm champagne border (`#DECDBA`) and Deep Slate text (`#1F1D1B`). Active hover induces a subtle background tint (`#F6F3EE`).
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background, Indigo Ink (`#1E2B45`) typography, underlined on hover with a 1px terracotta rule.
- **Monospace Action Variant:** Specialized mini-action buttons (e.g., `[COPY PROMPT]`, `[SYNC LINKEDIN]`) using JetBrains Mono with 1px border and letter-spacing `0.04em`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Match Score Indicator:** Contained pill with JetBrains Mono. 1px border. Background `#FAF5EE`, border `#E5DACB`, text `#A84F32` (e.g., `94.2% FIT`).
- **Skill / Domain Badges:** Soft rectangle (`0.25rem` radius), light warm stone fill (`#F3EFEA`), neutral text (`#3F3B36`), no border.
- **State Badges (Interview Scheduled, Analyzing):** Indigo tint (`#EEF2F6`) with deep indigo ink text (`#1E2B45`).

### Input Fields & Prompt Consoles
- **Interactive Prompt Console:** Elevated frosted glass docking bar anchored at the screen bottom. Enclosed by a 1px Champagne border (`#DECDBA`), featuring an expansive multi-line textarea with no native browser borders, a JetBrains Mono token counter, and an integrated solid Terracotta submission button.
- **Form Inputs:** Alabaster fill (`#FFFFFF`), 1px Stone border (`#E8E2D9`), espresso text. Focused state swaps border to Terracotta (`#C25E3E`) with a soft `0 0 0 2px rgba(194, 94, 62, 0.12)` ambient ring. Placeholder text styled in warm stone gray (`#8A8278`).

### Job Discovery & Synthesis Cards
- **Card Anatomy:** Crisp white surface (`#FFFFFF`), 1px champagne border (`#E8E2D9`), `0.5rem` corner radius, internal padding of `1.5rem`.
- **Card Header:** Role Title in Plus Jakarta Sans Headline-Sm alongside an inline monospace compensation ribbon (e.g., `$210K - $240K`).
- **AI Rationale Tray:** A nested container within the card styled in `#FDFCF9` with a left hairline accent border in Terracotta (`#C25E3E`), showcasing the AI agent's personalized strategic argument for the candidate.

### Checkboxes & Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes:** Clean `0.25rem` rounded square, 1px border (`#CFC6B8`). Active checked state fills with Terracotta (`#C25E3E`) featuring a crisp white geometric checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons:** Circular frame with active nested terracotta dot.
- **Switch:** Minimal track in `#E8E2D9`, thumb in white `#FFFFFF` with warm micro-shadow; toggles to Deep Indigo (`#1E2B45`) on activation.

### Lists & Activity Logs
- **Agent Reasoning Stream:** Linear chronological feed joined by a subtle 1px dashed stone guideline. Each step is punctuated by a monospace timestamp (`09:41:02`) and state dot (idle, searching, evaluating, finalized).
- **Dividers:** Fine 1px solid lines using `#E8E2D9` across all list items.
