## 3. Seasonality of Hiring, Graduations & Layoffs

Job seeking is not evenly distributed across the year. The platform must ramp up marketing and server capacity according to these specific waves:

```
[Q1: Jan - Feb]            [Q2: May - Jul]           [Q3: Aug - Oct]           [Q4: Nov - Dec]
================           ===============           ===============           ===============
Corporate Hiring Surge     University Exams          Graduates Enter Market    Corporate Layoffs
Budget Unlocks             & Graduations (MENA)      & Fall Hiring Ramp-up     Year-End Freezes
(Peak Monetization)        (User Acquisition)        (High Conversion)         (Restructuring)
```

### 3.1. University Graduation Season in MENA: **June, July, August**
* **The 3 Key Months:** **June, July, and August** are when graduation projects conclude, final university exams are graded, and graduation ceremonies take place across Egypt and the broader MENA region.
* **The Application Surge:** By **late July and August**, hundreds of thousands of new graduates flood LinkedIn and Wuzzuf to land their first internships or entry-level positions. This is the prime period for low-cost user acquisition, university ambassador programs, and starter credit packs.

### 3.2. Corporate Layoff Seasons: **November – December (Q4) & January (Q1)**
* **End of Year (November & December):** Companies conduct annual budget evaluations, performance cuts, and cost restructuring before the close of the fiscal year.
* **Early Q1 (January & February):** Companies implement post-review restructuring and department streamlining.
* **Impact on Product:** Laid-off mid-level to senior professionals enter the market with higher urgency, established purchasing power, and immediate willingness to pay for premium CV tailoring.

### 3.3. Corporate Hiring Peaks vs. Slumps
* **Major Hiring Surges:**
  * **January – March (Q1):** New annual operational budgets are approved; departments receive fresh headcount allocations.
  * **September – November (Fall):** Post-summer vacation recovery; hiring managers push to close vacancies before December budget freezes.
* **Hiring Slumps:**
  * **Ramadan & Eid Holidays:** Significant slowdown in interview loops and HR decision-making.
  * **Late December:** Corporate holiday freezes, key stakeholders on annual leave.

## 4. Competitor Landscape & Unsolved Flaws

### 4.1. The Competitors
* **Resume Keyword Matchers:** *Jobscan, SkillsSyncer*
* **AI Career Trackers / Builders:** *Teal, Rezi, Kickresume, Enhancv*
* **Automated Application Bots:** *LazyApply, Sonara, Simplify*

### 4.2. Flaws of Existing Tools (The Unmet Market Needs)
1. **The $29 – $49/Month Paywall:** Existing tools force recurring US-dollar subscriptions. For unemployed or emerging-market candidates (especially in Egypt/MENA facing currency inflation), paying $30/month (~1,500 EGP) is a non-starter.
2. **Zero Regional Support:** Existing platforms scrape and integrate exclusively with US/EU boards (Indeed, US LinkedIn, Glassdoor). They completely ignore regional engines like **Wuzzuf, Tanqeeb, or Bayt**.
3. **Generic AI Hallucinations & Keyword Stuffing:** Most competitors prompt LLMs to arbitrarily insert keywords into bullet points. This results in robotic, fabricated claims that fail technical human recruiter screening.
4. **Disconnected User Experience:** A user must find a job on LinkedIn, copy-paste it into Jobscan to check keywords, copy-paste it into ChatGPT to write a cover letter, and manually reformat Word/LaTeX documents.
5. **Account Banning Risk from Aggressive Bots:** Tools that auto-click and auto-apply get user LinkedIn accounts flagged and permanently restricted.
6. **Formatting Destruction:** Most builders force users into their proprietary web templates and export messy PDF/Word files that break ATS parsers or ruin bespoke LaTeX CV layouts.

### 4.3. Tailored's Core Differentiation
* **Hybrid Discovery + Tailoring:** Ingests directly from LinkedIn and Wuzzuf via stealth modules.
* **Fit-Gap Scoring Before Writing:** Computes skill match, experience gap, and compatibility score *first*, saving candidates from applying to mismatched roles.
* **High-Fidelity Document Generation:** Supports precise document diffs and LaTeX/clean PDF outputs without destroying the candidate's authentic design.
* **Micro-transaction Accessibility:** Low-barrier, local-currency credit packs without predatory subscription locks.

## 6. Team Strategy at the Pre-Revenue Stage

### 6.1. Do We Need a Team at the Beginning?
**Short answer: NO.**

* **Why:** The modern full-stack ecosystem (FastAPI, React, Docker, automated CI/CD, and frontier LLMs) allows a single skilled technical architect and product builder to deliver the entire MVP. Hiring non-technical staff or junior developers before achieving clear user demand burns capital, creates coordination friction, and slows down iteration.
* **Degree of Necessity:** **1 out of 10** for full-time hires. Everything should remain founder-led until consistent usage occurs.

### 6.2. When and What to Add (Phased Roadmap)

| Phase | Team Composition | Focus Area |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 1: Pre-Revenue / MVP** | **Solo Technical Architect / Product Lead** | Build backend, scrapers, AI pipeline, frontend UI, payment integration. |
| **Phase 2: Alpha Launch & Growth** | **Lead Architect + Part-Time Growth/Community Lead** | Drive campus networks, LinkedIn content, TikTok demo reels, and feedback collection. |
| **Phase 3: Post-Break-Even** | **Add 1 Customer Support / QA Specialist (Part-Time)** | Handle user queries, monitor failed scrapes, and manage community support. |

## 7. Architect & Designer Compensation Benchmark (EGP)

### 7.1. Market Realities in Egypt (Senior / Lead Architect)
* **Established Local Tech Companies:** **60,000 – 100,000 EGP/month** net salary.
* **VC-Funded Growth Startups (Regional):** **90,000 – 140,000 EGP/month** net salary.
* **International Remote Contracts:** **$2,500 – $4,500 USD/month** (~125,000 – 225,000 EGP).

### 7.2. Recommended Pre-Revenue Model
If the Project Architect is treated as an employee during the pre-revenue, self-funded/bootstrapped phase, draining 100k EGP/month in cash is unsustainable for a fledgling project. The recommended models are:

1. **Option 1: The Lean Living Stipend + Equity (Recommended)**
   * **Cash:** **30,000 – 45,000 EGP / month** net (covers cost of living and focus).
   * **Equity:** **20% – 35% founding equity stake**.
2. **Option 2: The Contracted Milestone Escalation (Pure Employee / Contractor)**
   * **Stage A (Pre-Revenue / Development):** **40,000 – 50,000 EGP / month**.
   * **Stage B (Post-Launch / Break-Even Trigger):** Contractual bump to **80,000 – 100,000 EGP / month** once monthly recurring gross revenue exceeds target thresholds.
3. **Option 3: Deferred Salary Accrual**
   * Receive a base stipend of 25,000 EGP/month now, with the remaining 50,000 EGP/month recorded as a founder loan/deferred debt to be repaid out of initial profits or first seed capital.

## 8. Pricing Structure: Option C (Credit Packs & Pay-Per-Service)

To ensure the product is **accessible to job seekers without leaving money on the table**, Tailored will use a frictionless credit-pack model, with free entry-tier incentives.

### 8.1. Credit Consumption Mechanics
* **1 Job Fit Evaluation & Gap Analysis:** Free (Hooks the user).
* **1 Tailored CV Regeneration:** 1 Credit.
* **1 Tailored Cover Letter:** 1 Credit.
* **1 Complete Job Package (Tailored CV + Cover Letter + LaTeX/PDF Export):** 2 Credits.

## 9. Break-Even Analysis & Timeline

### 9.1. Cost Scenarios for Break-Even Calculation

* **Scenario 1 (Ultra-Lean: Tech Infrastructure Only, Zero Salary):**
  * Monthly Burn: **~4,000 EGP / month** ($80 USD).
  * Break-Even Point: **~23 Starter Packs (49 EGP)** or **~25 Job Hunter Packs**.
  * Timeline: **Month 1 post-launch**.

* **Scenario 2 (Sustainable: Tech Infrastructure + Architect Pre-Revenue Stipend):**
  * Tech Expenses: 5,000 EGP
  * Architect Living Stipend: 40,000 EGP
  * Payment Processing & Buffer (10%): 5,000 EGP
  * **Total Monthly Target to Break Even:** **50,000 EGP / month** (~$1,000 USD).

### 9.2. How to Generate 50,000 EGP / Month:

$$\text{Required Units} = \frac{\text{Monthly Burn (50,000 EGP)}}{\text{Net Profit per Pack (~160 EGP)}} \approx \mathbf{312 \text{ Job Hunter Packs / month}}$$

Or a realistic blended sales mix per month:
* 200 Starter Packs (49 EGP) = 9,800 EGP
* 180 Job Hunter Packs (179 EGP) = 32,220 EGP
* 30 Sprint Packs (399 EGP) = 11,970 EGP
* **Total Gross:** **53,990 EGP / month** (Break-Even Achieved).

### 9.3. Break-Even Timeline Roadmap

```
Month 1: MVP Finalization & Alpha Launch (0 EGP revenue | Burn: 45k EGP)
Month 2: Soft Launch (Egypt Student & Tech Communities) -> ~50-80 sales (~12k EGP)
Month 3: Viral Organic Demos + Graduation Season Ramp -> ~150 sales (~28k EGP)
Month 4: Referral Engine + First Gulf Users -> ~250 sales (~45k EGP)
Month 5: SUSTAINABLE BREAK-EVEN REACHED (350+ sales | >55,000 EGP/mo)
```

* **Expected Break-Even Timeline:** **4 to 5 Months from public launch** under standard organic execution, or **Month 2 to 3** if launched right before or during the university graduation wave (May–July).
